How to get the two files from Substack
  1. The export .zip (needed). Open Settings, find Import / Export, and under “Export your data” choose New export. Download the .zip and leave it zipped.
  2. The subscriber .csv (adds the sign-up source signal). Open Subscribers, click the three dots above “All subscribers”, choose Export, keep “My entire email list” and “All columns”, then “Download last export”.
  3. Drop both files here together, or use Choose files and select both.

Most important metrics

Point at or tap a box to read it. Each box opens the tab named on it. The boxes follow the order of the tabs.

  • An open here is an email open. Reading in the Substack app is not logged, so app-only readers look like non-openers.
  • Rates are divided by emails delivered, so they can be higher than the open rate on your Substack dashboard.
  • People who unsubscribed are counted in the first-email and cohort findings, not in the list count.
